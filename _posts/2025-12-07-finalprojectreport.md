Description:

I created a electronic headband where it uses the light sensor to detect if it is the morning or night, and given what time of day it is it lights up and plays music. For instance, in the morning, the lights are half-dim and play the theme song to the Amelie movie and then, at night, the lights are fully on and play a Billie Holiday song. All I need to do its connect the two flaps and the music starts to play and the LED lights up! 

The light sensor and the conducive patch are the inputs, and the LEDS and the buzzer are the outputs. 


Motivation: 
The idea for the Wake Up & Wind Down Headband arrived when I was making my bed and doing my skin care in the morning before class. I always wear a headband because I dislike having my hair in my face while cleaning and relaxing, as well as, I always have music playing in my room. In the morning, I play upbeat indie rock and at night, soft jazz. Therefore, I thought having a headband that combined all of these quirks from my daily routine would be practical and fun!

3 Tips for Past Self: 
I would have made a third paper prototype in terms of how I want the seams/thread to show because I ran into some spatial issues in pursuit of keeping the stitches hidden. My second tip would be to use cotton fabric because I used mainly a slippery jersey knit and it was very hard to maintain a straight and evenly spaced stitch. Cotton would have saved alot of headaches! My last tip would be to go to office hours sooner rather than later! I am very grateful for Prof. Howley's help after class, and I wish I went sooner. I think if I went sooner, I could have brainstormed a couple final project ideas. All in all, I am very proud of the work I did!
